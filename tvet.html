Technical & Vocational Skills

Equipping youth with practical skills to create sustainable livelihoods and entrepreneurial opportunities.

Tailoring

Our tailoring program teaches young people how to sew, design, and create high-quality products. Students gain hands-on experience making bags, school uniforms, and other items.

  • Hands-on sewing workshops
  • Bag production for sale and practice
  • Entrepreneurship and business guidance

Help Us Complete Our Training Shelter

We are currently constructing a community shelter that will support vocational training programs such as tailoring. This space will provide a safe environment where community members can learn practical skills.
