About Us

Skills for Change Zambia Limited is a youth-focused organization dedicated to empowering young people through education, skills development and environmental action.

We believe that informed, skilled and environmentally conscious youth are the foundation of a stronger and more sustainable Zambia.

Why We Exist

  • Promote youth health & wellbeing
  • Provide practical vocational skills
  • Drive climate action initiatives
